PT Istana Surya Perkasa · 2016

Building the People Foundation

As HR Business Partner, I supported a manufacturing company’s growth from 30 to 250 employees by putting in place the people systems the business was missing.

Situation

  • No company regulations, SOPs, job descriptions, or organization structure
  • Headcount growing fast, with new departments opening to match business processes

What I did

  • Set up company regulations, SOPs, job descriptions, and the organization structure
  • Scaled the recruitment process to a capacity of up to 250 employees
  • Implemented an HRIS as the backbone for alignment, supporting lower budget on general company needs
  • Designed and implemented KPIs to track employee performance
  • Supported product SNI licensing, part of the strategy toward ISO 9001 certification

Result

  • Headcount from 30 to 250
  • A complete, structured HR foundation
  • Employee performance visible through KPIs
